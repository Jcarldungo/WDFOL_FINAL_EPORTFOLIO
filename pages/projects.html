WeePlay Therapy Center

A production website for a pediatric therapy clinic in Mabalacat, Pampanga — my first real client project, currently live and serving actual families. WeePlay provides Occupational, Speech & Language, and Physical Therapy plus SPED tutorials, and the site had to communicate warmth and trust to parents while staying fast and accessible on the low-end phones most of them browse on.

Built from scratch with no framework: hand-written semantic HTML, a custom CSS design system, and vanilla JavaScript. Deployed on Vercel with continuous deployment from GitHub.

Tech Stack: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, Vercel

  • Real client site for a working business — not a practice project
  • Custom CSS design system with themed tokens, no UI framework
  • Mobile-first responsive layout tuned for low-end phones
  • Accessible by design — skip links, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation
  • Click-to-call, Facebook Messenger, and inquiry contact paths
  • Photo gallery of the therapy space, services, and parent reviews
  • Open Graph & SEO meta for clean link previews when shared

gastos

An offline-first expense tracker built for logging a purchase in three taps, even with no signal. No framework — vanilla JS talking to Vercel serverless functions over a Neon Postgres database, with a service worker queueing entries made offline and syncing them once the connection returns.

The interesting part was fitting it onto a free plan: Vercel's Hobby tier caps a deployment at 12 serverless functions, and the app's ~32 routes (auth, transactions, budgets, bills, goals, splitting expenses with friends) each started as its own file. Consolidating them into 10 thin dispatcher functions — routed by a single vercel.json rewrite table shared by production and local dev — got it under the cap with zero change to any URL or response shape.

Tech Stack: Vanilla JavaScript, Vercel Serverless Functions, Neon PostgreSQL, Service Worker/PWA, Web Push

  • Card balances derived from the transaction ledger, not typed in — including transfers between cards
  • Offline outbox: entries logged with no signal queue locally and sync automatically once back online
  • Split an expense with friends and settle up later, with itemized per-person history
  • Web Push alerts for bills coming due and budgets crossing 80%, via a nightly Vercel Cron
  • Money kept as strings end to end (never a JS float) and dates read back as calendar strings, not Date objects, to avoid timezone drift
